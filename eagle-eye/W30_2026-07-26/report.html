This Week’s Take

Executive Summary

Ⅰ The Lede Is a Weekend De-escalation

After thirteen straight nights of U.S. strikes on Iran drove Brent above $100 and flipped the Fed conversation from “how many cuts” to “will they hike,” the shooting paused for a second straight day (Sat 07-25 / Sun 07-26) as Iran–Oman and Pakistan/China-brokered diplomacy moved forward. Oil gapped sharply lower on the Sunday reopen (Brent ~−5% to ~$91–92, a weekend futures proxy) and U.S. equity futures firmed (Dow +0.5%, S&P +0.6%, Nasdaq-100 +1.2%). This is fragile — the U.S. naval blockade remains fully operational and the strait is not cleared, so the premium can snap back on any headline — but as of this writing the tape is set to open risk-on, with a lower oil print easing the inflation impulse that had been the week’s dominant macro fear.

🕊️ The biggest driver into the Jul 27 open — energy/defensives/gold likely to give back some of last week’s safe-haven bid; keep the Iran hedge on.

Ⅱ The Dominant Structural Story — the AI-Infrastructure Trade Cracked

U.S. equities logged a second consecutive weekly loss (S&P 500 −0.6% to 7,411.98, Nasdaq −2.0% to 24,975.82; the SOX semiconductor index −4.25% on the week) as the market began punishing AI capital spending rather than rewarding it. The pivotal catalyst was Alphabet’s July 22 report — a clean beat (revenue $119.8B, +24%; Google Cloud +82%) that the market sold hard on a raised 2026 capex outlook, with the “Magnificent Seven” shedding roughly $800B in a single Thursday session. Tesla fell −17.8% on its own ~$25B capex plan. Money rotated out of mega-cap tech into defensives, energy, small caps and — the cleanest relative-strength signal of the cycle — cybersecurity (PANW +79%, CRWD +59% YTD held near highs while chips broke).

📈 A regime shift, not a breadth breakdown — NVDA’s relative resilience (−0.9% vs SOX −4.25%) says the market is discriminating, not dumping.

Ⅲ Everything Funnels Into a Loaded, Two-Sided Week

The FOMC decides Wednesday 07-29 — Chair Warsh’s second meeting — with a hold still the base case (~62–65%) but hike odds having tripled to ~35–38% (from 10.7% on 07-15) on the oil-and-inflation spike; Core PCE printed 3.4%, the hottest since October 2023. Then GDP and PCE land Thursday, MSFT/META report Wednesday and AAPL/AMZN Thursday, and Iran headlines run underneath all of it. Rates sold off across the curve (10Y 4.69%, +14bp; it topped 4.7% intraday Thursday, highest since Jan 2025), the dollar firmed, the yen sank to a 40-year low near ¥164, gold sits in a bear market (down ~27% from its January record) yet bounced +1% on the week, copper is near record highs, and Bitcoin is stuck in a “volatility trap” near $64K.

🏛️ The most genuinely two-sided Fed meeting in a while — and the weekend oil gap-down is the wildcard that could pull hike odds back.

Ⅳ The Setup — Risk-On Into a Gauntlet

Trade the weekend gap, but keep the Iran hedge on. Sunday futures and the ~−5% oil move argue for a risk-on Monday and a partial unwind of the energy/defensive/gold safe-haven trade — yet the blockade is intact and the strait isn’t cleared, so size for two-way risk into Wednesday. The AI-capex regime shift is the trade of the quarter: until proven otherwise, favor AI monetizers and power/infrastructure with pricing power over pure capex sinks and memory — and MSFT/META (Wed) and AAPL/AMZN (Thu) are the referendum. Respect the two-sided FOMC, watch Thursday’s PCE as the swing factor, and treat cybersecurity as where relative strength lives if the semi de-rating continues.

🧭 Risk-on into Monday, but into a gauntlet — watch the oil open, the Wednesday Fed, and whether the market keeps punishing AI capex.
