## Positioning & Thesis Update

1. **Trade the weekend gap, but keep the Iran hedge on.** Sunday futures and the ~-5% oil move argue for a risk-on Monday and a partial unwind of the energy/defensive/gold safe-haven trade. But the blockade is intact and the strait isn't cleared — the oil premium can snap back on one headline. Don't chase energy shorts aggressively into a still-live conflict; size for two-way risk into Wednesday.
2. **The AI-capex regime shift is the trade of the quarter.** The market has flipped from rewarding AI spend to punishing it (GOOG, TSLA). **MSFT/META (Wed) and AAPL/AMZN (Thu) are the referendum.** Until proven otherwise, favor **AI monetizers and power/infrastructure with pricing power over pure capex sinks and memory.** NVDA's relative resilience (-0.9% vs SOX -4.25%) says the market is discriminating, not dumping.
3. **Cybersecurity is the cleanest rotation-within-tech.** PANW/CRWD/OKTA held near highs as chips broke — a Tier-1 thesis working precisely *because* the AI-hardware trade cracked. This is where relative strength lives if the semi de-rating continues.
4. **Rates: respect the two-sided FOMC.** A hold is base case but ~35–38% hike odds are real; 10Y near 4.7% and a firm dollar reflect it. **Credit is calm (HY ~277 bp)** — the tell to watch is whether HY widens on any oil re-spike. Thursday's PCE is the swing factor.
5. **FX: the yen at a 40-year low is a coiled spring.** ¥164 has revived intervention warnings; a hawkish Fed + dovish BOJ + energy-import bill keep pressure on. EM oil-importers (INR, TRY) are the most exposed to any Hormuz re-escalation.
6. **Commodities theses intact:** copper near records (FCX/SCCO), lithium recovering (+30% YTD; ALB/SQM/PLS), uranium steady (CCJ/LEU/UUUU) — structural-deficit stories unbothered by the oil noise. Gold's bear market (-27% from the Jan peak) is the notable de-rating; a hawkish Fed keeps it capped, but it remains a hedge if Iran re-escalates.

ETH-ETF inflows bucking BTC's outflows is a subtle relative signal, but this is a weak, Fear-driven tape — respect the $62.5K BTC support and don't confuse accumulation-by-treasuries with price momentum.
