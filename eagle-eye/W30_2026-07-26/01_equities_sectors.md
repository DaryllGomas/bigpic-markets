## Weekend Developments

**MATERIAL WEEKEND EVENT — U.S.–Iran de-escalation.** This is the most important item for Monday's open.

- **U.S. and Iran paused military strikes for a second straight day** (Sat 07-25 and Sun 07-26) in the Persian Gulf, with the Trump administration signaling the pause will let diplomatic talks progress. Deputy foreign ministers of **Iran and Oman** held "constructive" talks in Tehran on **Strait of Hormuz** safe-passage mechanisms, though there was **no change yet to maritime traffic status** through the strait ([CBS News live 07-26](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-hormuz-middle-east/); [CNN live 07-26](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/26/world/live-news/iran-war-trump); [Fox News live 07-26](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/us-iran-peace-strait-hormuz-talks-strikes-hold-july-26)).
- **Oil eased on the pause.** September Brent crude fell **-4.9% to ~$92.02** early Sunday ([ClickOrlando/AP 07-26](https://www.clickorlando.com/business/2026/07/26/oil-prices-ease-after-us-and-iran-pause-their-attacks/)). For reference, Friday settles were **Brent ~$96.88** and **WTI ~$88.96** ([CNBC 07-26 recap](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/26/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). Oil had spiked above $100 (Brent) intraweek and neared ~$120 at the war's peak earlier in 2026.
- **Sunday-night U.S. futures rallied** on the de-escalation and lower oil: **Dow futures +244 pts (+0.5%), S&P 500 futures +0.6%, Nasdaq-100 futures +1.2%** ([CNBC 07-26](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/26/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)).

**Context on the conflict (historical, dated):** A two-week U.S.–Iran ceasefire was struck in **April 2026**; the U.S. struck Iran again **~July 8, 2026** in retaliation for attacks on three commercial ships in the Strait of Hormuz, and President Trump declared the ceasefire "over," reversing sanctions relief and re-igniting oil volatility ([Al Jazeera 07-08](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/8/oil-prices-surge-as-us-strikes-iran-reversing-fall-to-pre-war-levels); [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/5960020-iran-ceasefire-gas-prices-strait-of-hormuz/)). This weekend's pause is the newest turn.

**Setup for the week ahead (07-27 to 07-31):** A heavy macro/earnings gauntlet — **FOMC decision Wed 07-29** (per market-structure calendar; Jul 28–29 meeting), plus **Microsoft and Meta after the close Wed 07-29** and **Apple and Amazon after the close Thu 07-30** ([TradingKey week-ahead](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262054300-weekly-preview-fed-apple-microsoft-meta-amazon-earnings-reports-tradingkey); [FXLeaders 07-26](https://www.fxleaders.com/news/2026/07/26/top-earnings-to-watch-this-week-july-27-31-what-to-expect-from-msft-meta-aapl-amzn-ko-ba-and-f/)).
