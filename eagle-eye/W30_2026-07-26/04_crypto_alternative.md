## Regulation & Institutional

**Regulation — CLARITY Act (market structure) is the key catalyst:**
- Senate Republicans released an **updated CLARITY Act draft on 2026-07-22**, combining the Senate Banking and Agriculture Committee bills, revising ethics provisions and decentralized-activity exemptions.
- It creates a 3-tier digital-asset classification and gives the **CFTC exclusive jurisdiction over spot markets for digital commodities**, defining how much of the market falls under securities law vs CFTC oversight.
- Status: on the Senate calendar with **no floor vote scheduled**; the **2026-08-10** state work period is treated as the practical deadline. Needs 60 votes — key Democrats (Alsobrooks, Gallego) are conditioning support on tougher ethics provisions. Passage odds this session are uncertain.

## Weekend Developments

**No single catastrophic weekend liquidation event, but several live threads:**

1. **Bitcoin fork drama (August 2026) — the distinctive story.** Two forks converge next month and are already feeding the "volatility trap":
   - **eCash hard fork** (Paul Sztorc / Drivechain), scheduled at block height **964,000** in August 2026. A "clean reboot" that **reassigns Satoshi's dormant coins** and airdrops new coins 1:1 to BTC holders at the snapshot. Widely condemned as "theft." Because it retains SHA-256 and lowers difficulty at genesis, it risks "miner wars" that could disrupt hash rate / slow BTC block times.
   - **BIP-110 soft fork**, activation targeted ~block **961,632** in August 2026 — bans "spam" (NFTs, ordinals, runes), reigniting the maximalist-vs-ordinals split.
   - First-ever fork where the biggest holders are **ETF sponsors, corporate treasuries (Strategy), and custodians** (>2M BTC). Notably, IBIT's prospectus says the trust will **permanently abandon** rights to forked/airdropped assets — so ETF holders likely forgo any eCash airdrop.

2. **Bridge hacks (midweek, within window):** ~$31.5M combined across two cross-chain bridges — **AFX Trade** (2026-07-22, ~$24.15M USDC drained on Arbitrum via 5 compromised validator keys) and the **Verus–Ethereum bridge** (2026-07-23, ~$7.54M → 3,916 ETH). Also within the month: Ostium (~$18M via compromised oracle key), Allbridge Core (~$1.65M, 07-19/20), BarnBridge governance attack (~$776K, 07-15).

3. **Leverage flush:** ~$243M in 24h liquidations around 2026-07-25 — ~$214M longs vs ~$29M shorts (BTC ~97% long-liquidated, ETH ~91% long) — a leverage-clearing profile on downside wicks, not a short squeeze.

4. **Macro overhang into the weekend:** US–Iran tensions, oil >$100, ~18-month-high Treasury yields; all eyes on FOMC (07-28/29, expected hold) and the CLARITY Act clock.
