### What drove it

The **2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis** dominates everything. Per the [crisis chronology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis), the closure began **2026-02-28** after coordinated US/Israeli strikes on Iran; Brent peaked at **$126/bbl** during the March 2026 spike. As of **2026-08-02** the strait is on **Day 155** of closure with throughput at roughly **11% of pre-crisis levels** — about **10 vessel transits on 2026-07-23 versus ~88/day normally**, and **71 tankers running dark** (AIS disabled) ([straits.live tracker, 2026-08-02 22:55Z](https://straits.live/)).

Week-specific catalysts:
- **2026-07-27:** The US **paused airstrikes on Iran after 13 days**; Iranian and Omani deputy foreign ministers met in Tehran on Hormuz shipping. Iran explicitly denied it was negotiating bilaterally with Washington ([Japan Times, 2026-07-27](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/07/27/world/politics/iran-halt-strikes-us-bombing-pause/); [CNBC, 2026-07-27](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz.html)). This pause is what deflated the risk premium and drove the week's decline.
- **2026-07-31:** Iran's Revolutionary Guards **stopped two tankers** from transiting Hormuz and **four others changed course** (Fars News, cited by Reuters) — the intraday catalyst for Friday's +1.3% bounce.
- Houthis threatened vessels transiting **Bab el-Mandeb**, and drone strikes threatened gas vessels near the **Suez Canal** — the two alternate routes are also compromised.

### The yen intervention — the week's biggest FX event

This is the story. **USD/JPY had pushed to 40-year lows for the yen around 164** before **suspected official intervention on Thursday 2026-07-30** drove the pair from **above 163 to below 158 — a ~3.3% yen surge** in the New York session, the largest since the 2024 intervention rounds.

Three things make this different from prior episodes:
1. **The US Treasury informed banks it might transact to support the yen** — this reads as **coordinated/sanctioned intervention**, not unilateral MOF action. That is a materially higher-conviction signal than Tokyo acting alone.
2. **South Korea was also suspected of intervening** in the same window.
3. It landed **one day before the BOJ decision**. On **2026-07-31 the BOJ held at 1.00% in an 8–1 vote**, with **Hajime Takata dissenting for 1.25%**. USD/JPY promptly **retraced to ~160.37** — i.e. the market gave back most of the intervention gain within 24 hours.

The read-through: officials reportedly discussed **raising rates faster than markets expect** specifically because yen weakness has not responded to verbal intervention or large-scale yen buying. Note that **164 was never an announced line** — Tokyo frames its concern around *speed*, so treat 163–164 as a sensitivity zone, not a committed trigger. Absent a credible BOJ tightening path, official yen buying is unlikely to produce a lasting reversal. ([MUFG Research, 2026-07-31](https://www.mufgresearch.com/fx/asia-fx-talk-suspected-jpy-intervention-ahead-of-boj-policy-meeting-31-july-2026/); [Exchange Rates UK, 2026-07-31](https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/news/46676/2026-07-31-boj-holds-rates-japanese-yen-gives-back-intervention-gains.html); [CNBC, 2026-07-30](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/dollar-steadies-as-fed-holds-rates-us-strikes-iran.html))

## Weekend Developments

Three material events since Friday's 4pm ET close. **All three are bearish crude.**

### 1. Trump cancels planned Iran strikes — Saturday 2026-08-01

Trump posted on Truth Social **Saturday evening 2026-08-01** that he had been "asked by Iran, and other Middle Eastern Countries, to hold off any attack in that the perimeters of a deal has been agreed to," and that the deal "would include the **Immediate, Complete, and Total OPENING OF THE HORMUZ STRAIT**, and an end to Iran's nuclear threat." Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman had called Trump urging negotiation; Israel reportedly signed on ([Fortune, 2026-08-01](https://fortune.com/2026/08/01/trump-iran-strikes-mideast-allies-emerging-strait-of-hormuz/); [CNBC, 2026-08-02](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/02/trump-planned-attack-on-iran-canceled-after-reaching-outline-of-deal.html); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/middle-east/state-department-urges-americans-consider-leaving-middle-east-region-rcna590396)).

**The catch — this is contested.** Per the [straits.live event log](https://straits.live/), **Iran publicly denied any reopening agreement on 2026-08-01, calling media reports "false."** By **2026-08-02 22:42Z** Iran was describing its **negotiations with Oman** (not the US) as "in final stages." Denuclearisation talks were reported set to begin **Monday 2026-08-03**. Historical context for calibration: a **14-point MOU was signed by Trump and Pezeshkian on 2026-06-17** (the Islamabad MOU) that already promised Hormuz reopening plus sanctions relief — **and the strait is still closed 46 days later.** Its 60-day negotiating window expires around **2026-08-16**. This is at minimum the third announced Hormuz reopening of 2026 (April ceasefire, June MOU, now August). Fade accordingly.

### 2. OPEC+ September hike — Sunday 2026-08-02

Covered in full under Energy. Headline: **+188 kb/d for September, completing the 1.65 mb/d voluntary-cut rollback, with increases paused from Q4 2026.** Supply is being added into a market that may be about to get its chokepoint back.

### 3. LNG carrier *Gaslog Shanghai* disabled — 2026-07-31/2026-08-01

The **Bermuda-flagged LNG tanker *Gaslog Shanghai***, laden with **Qatari LNG loaded at Ras Laffan** and transiting the **Omani coastal route southbound**, was **struck in the engine room by an unknown projectile and lost all power**. Sources date this **2026-07-31** (Wikipedia chronology) or **2026-08-01** (Bloomberg) — **the exact date is unresolved**; it straddles the Friday close either way. Two further tankers were struck and disabled on the southern route overnight into 2026-08-01, with four more turning back after warnings.

Analysts read this together with an earlier-in-the-week forced turnaround of a separate Qatar-linked LNG carrier as a **deliberate signal targeting Qatari LNG specifically**, not isolated incidents ([Bloomberg, 2026-08-01](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-01/tanker-carrying-qatari-lng-struck-while-transiting-hormuz); [Maritime Executive](https://maritime-executive.com/article/lng-carrier-hit-by-iranian-attack-in-strait-of-hormuz); [Wikipedia chronology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis)).
