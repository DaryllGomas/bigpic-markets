**The three things that mattered:**

1. **FOMC (Wed 2026-07-29) — a hawkish hold under new Chair Kevin Warsh.** The FOMC voted **9-3 to hold the fed funds target at 3.50–3.75%**, with **Hammack, Kashkari, and Logan dissenting in favor of a 25bp HIKE** ([Federal Reserve statement, 07-29-2026](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm); [CNBC, 07-29-2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/fed-rate-decision-july-2026.html)). Warsh's presser explicitly rejected any tolerance for above-target inflation and framed tariffs, the Middle East war, supply-chain disruption, and the AI capex surge as persistent **"economic shocks"** ([Motley Fool, 07-29-2026](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/29/2-words-fed-chair-kevin-warsh-stock-market-tumble/)). Equities broke hard: **SPX -1.52% to 7,316, DJIA -2.19% to 51,594, Nasdaq Composite -1.74% to 24,443** on 07-29 ([Motley Fool, 07-29-2026](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/07/29/stock-market-today-july-29-stocks-slide-on-hawkish-fed-and-increased-middle-east-tensions/)). The **10-year Treasury finished the week at 4.75%** and the **30-year at 5.23%, a 19-year high** ([Advisor Perspectives Treasury Snapshot, 07-31-2026](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/dshort/updates/2026/07/31/treasury-yields-snapshot-july-31-2026)). *Note the regime shift: the market is no longer pricing cuts — it is pricing hike risk.*

2. **The semiconductor unwind finished its worst month since 2008.** The **SOX fell 21% in July, its worst month since October 2008**, erasing **$2.2 trillion** in market cap; it is **-23% from its June 22 peak**. Nearly half of July's sessions saw 4%+ swings and *all 22 sessions* had 2%+ intraday moves. July damage: **TSMC -15% (-$380B), Micron -29% (-$374B), Intel -35% (-$247B)** — though **NVDA and AVGO posted July gains**. A record **$12B flowed into semiconductor ETFs in the final week alone** ([Fortune, 08-02-2026](https://fortune.com/2026/08/02/wall-street-ai-trade-chip-stocks-volatility-sox-selloff/)).

3. **The trigger was a China chip-tooling shock.** On **2026-07-27/28**, reports emerged that Shanghai Aishengna Electronic Technology Group had begun **mass production of immersion DUV lithography systems**, with first deliveries to SMIC, Hua Hong, and CXMT this year. **ASML fell as much as 8% on 07-28**; BESI -8.5%, Soitec -5%, Infineon -3% ([Bloomberg, 07-27-2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/asml-slides-after-report-of-china-beginning-duv-tool-production); [TrendForce, 07-28-2026](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/07/28/news-china-reportedly-starts-mass-producing-immersion-duv-tools-smic-hua-hong-cxmt-deliveries-expected-this-year/)). **Scale caveat:** China targets ~5 machines in 2026 and ~20 in 2027 versus ASML's ~130 DUV immersion capacity for 2026, and some critical components are still imported from Japan ([CNBC, 07-28-2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/china-chipmaking-duv-tool-asml-explained.html)). The market traded the headline, not the math.

**The Korea blow-up is the most extreme datapoint of the cycle.** South Korea's **Kospi ended July down 22%** — the steepest month since the GFC — after **erasing ~₩864.5 trillion** and triggering **circuit breakers four times in the month, including consecutive sessions on 07-28 and 07-29** (a first). Retail investors had poured ~₩78 trillion (~$54.2B) into Kospi shares in May–June, much of it via newly-launched **single-stock leveraged ETFs** on Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix (together >50% of the index), which mechanically amplified the decline into margin calls. Then on **Friday 07-31 the Kospi closed +18%, a record single-day surge**, with **Samsung +27% and SK Hynix +30%** ([Fortune, 08-02-2026](https://fortune.com/2026/08/02/kospi-rout-korea-stock-market-volatility-sk-hynix-samsung-ai-leveraged-etf/); [KED Global, 07-29-2026](https://www.kedglobal.com/korean-stock-market/newsView/ked202607290011); [Al Jazeera, 07-29-2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/29/south-koreas-stock-market-plunges-as-ai-driven-boom-fades)). *Source spread note: outlets cite July Kospi losses of 22% / 23% / 29% / 33% depending on whether they measure to month-end (after the 07-31 rebound) or to the intra-month trough. The **-22% month-end figure** is the one to carry.*

**Also in-window and under-covered:** the **Section 232 100% tariff on patented pharmaceuticals and APIs took effect 2026-07-31** for the 17 large pharma importers named in Annex III of the April 2 proclamation (all other importers: 2026-09-29). Generics and biosimilars — >90% of US prescriptions — remain exempt for now ([Mallory Group, importer guide](https://www.mallorygroup.com/blog-posts/100-duties-two-deadlines-section-232-pharmaceutical-tariffs-take-effect-july-31-2026); [PharmExec, "Preparing for the July 31 Pharma Tariff Deadline"](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/preparing-july-31-tariff-deadline)).
